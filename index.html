Questions

Common questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many doors and how spread out the neighborhoods are. Request a quote and we'll give you a flat price before anything gets hung.

Do I need to supply the door hangers?

No. We design and print them for you, and it's included in your price. Send us your logo, phone number and offer, and we'll send a proof for you to approve.

Do you put them in mailboxes?

No. Mailboxes are federal property, so we only hang on front doors and door handles.

Will my hanger be next to a competitor's?

No. We only take one company per trade on a route.

How do I pay?

Once we agree on a price, we email you an invoice through Stripe. Pay by card or bank transfer from the email, or use the Pay your invoice button on this page.

What areas do you cover?

Knoxville, Lenoir City, Farragut, Maryville, Oak Ridge and surrounding East Tennessee towns. Ask if you're farther out.
